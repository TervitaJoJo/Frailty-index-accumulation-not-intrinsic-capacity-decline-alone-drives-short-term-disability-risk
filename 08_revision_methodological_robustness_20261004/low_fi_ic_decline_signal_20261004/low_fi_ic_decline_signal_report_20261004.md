# Low-baseline-FI IC-decline-only signal audit

Run date: 2026-10-04  
Development cohorts: ELSA, CHARLS and HRS  
Primary baseline-FI threshold: FI <0.125  
Outcome: terminal-window incident non-walking ADL/IADL disability

## Estimand

Within participants with low baseline deficit burden, the primary contrast was IC decline only versus preserved IC/low accumulation. The state was defined from the same baseline-to-intermediate primary-sign rule used in the manuscript. Models adjusted for age, sex, education, baseline FI and cohort. Risk tables used all participants with known outcomes; regression models used complete covariate records. The analysis was aggregate-only.

## Main result

The low-FI group contained 5,561 participants with known outcomes and 440 disability events. Disability risk was 5.10% in preserved/low accumulation, 5.20% in IC decline only, 9.51% in FI accumulation only and 10.25% in coupled change.

In the covariate-adjusted pooled model, IC decline only versus preserved/low accumulation had OR=0.918 (95% CI 0.633-1.330; P=0.651). FI accumulation only had OR=1.654 (1.179-2.319; P=0.0035), and coupled change had OR=1.708 (1.235-2.361; P=0.0012). The coupled-versus-FI-only contrast was OR=2.825 (1.525-5.230; P=0.0010).

The cohort-specific IC decline-only estimates were OR=1.203 (0.727-1.991; P=0.472) in CHARLS, OR=0.687 (0.351-1.344; P=0.273) in ELSA and OR=0.543 (0.177-1.665; P=0.285) in HRS. A three-cohort random-effects summary was OR=0.880 (95% CI 0.554-1.398), with I-squared=25.1%; the estimate remained compatible with no association.

## Effect-modification check

The global state-by-baseline-FI-band interaction across the three descriptive bands was not supported: likelihood-ratio statistic 8.43 on 6 degrees of freedom, P=0.208. The IC decline-only versus preserved contrast was OR=1.074 (0.806-1.432) in FI 0.125-<0.25 and OR=1.281 (1.065-1.542) in FI >=0.25, but these exploratory stratum-specific estimates do not establish a monotonic interaction and the global test was not significant.

## Interpretation

These data do not support the proposed claim that IC decline only provides an independent early-warning signal among participants with low baseline FI. In this primary low-FI analysis, IC decline-only risk was close to the preserved state and the confidence interval was wide. The clear low-FI separation occurred when FI accumulation was present, either alone or coupled with IC decline. The manuscript should therefore avoid claiming that IC decline has demonstrated lead-time value before FI accumulation.

The result remains informative for the complementary-construct discussion: IC decline may describe a different biological or functional dimension, but this dataset does not show that the IC decline-only state translates into higher short-term disability risk when deficit burden is low. A stronger early-warning claim would require repeated IC and FI measurements that establish temporal precedence, followed by a later outcome or later FI transition.
